# CHANTIER — Business case

## Intended customer

Plumbing and trade contractors managing both short service calls and multi-day projects. The product aims to connect office assignments with field progress, verified hours and job costs.

No company is represented as an actual client, pilot participant or endorser.

| Owner's question | V1 demonstration |
| --- | --- |
| Who is assigned where? | Crew schedule and employee assignments |
| Which work is blocked? | Field logs, blockers and punch items |
| Which hours need approval? | Job-linked time submissions and review |
| What has the project cost? | Approved labour/material costs versus budget |
| Is the work complete? | Completion submission and manager decision |

## Validation before commercial use

Confirm the buyer's present workflow and most costly exception. Measure schedule conflicts, missing updates, review delay, unresolved blockers and cost-record completeness against an agreed baseline.

A company subscription is a commercial hypothesis; pricing and willingness to pay are not validated. A pilot needs agreed users/sites, support, success measures, data handling, export and exit terms. The local development build must first gain authenticated access, operational hardening and device validation.

All current data is simulated. No customer benefits or revenue are claimed.
