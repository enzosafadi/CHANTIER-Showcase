# CHANTIER — Designed for contractors with little time

## Intended daily experience

Independent painters, handymen and small renovation crews need a clear next action while working on a house. The default owner view is Today, Jobs and Costs; detailed team controls remain optional.

Implemented: three-field job entry, job-card notes, owner-recorded solo expenses and completion, with separate safeguards for team work. Quotes, customer balances, payment records, photos and offline behavior remain planned.

## Research direction

- [Jobber mobile basics](https://getjobber.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/7061327071639-Jobber-App-Basics) supports studying day-oriented work and contextual actions.
- [Jobber quotes](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/quotes-in-the-jobber-app/) informs a future reusable quote-to-job workflow.
- [JobTread daily logs](https://app.jobtread.com/help/daily-logs) illustrates updates attached to a job.
- [NN/G progressive disclosure](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/) informs the separation of frequent actions and specialist tools.
- [NN/G form-effort framework](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/eas-framework-simplify-forms/) informs removing unnecessary questions and reusing known details.

These sources guide design; they do not prove customer demand or comparative speed.

## Targets to validate

Find the next job within 5 seconds; add a basic job within 60 seconds; add a short note within 15 seconds after opening the job; record an expense within 30 seconds. These are proposed targets, not measured results.

Test unassisted with solo contractors and small crews on actual phones, including people uncomfortable with software. Record completion, time, errors, hesitation and whether cost and customer balance are understood correctly.

Readability, reliable saves, correction/recovery, French/English support and interrupted-network behavior matter alongside a short interface. The local prototype is not a commercial or store-ready app.
